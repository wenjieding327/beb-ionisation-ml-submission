2026-09-06 evidence-corrected release v2
=====================================

Status: corrected inputs, all three task heads, nested molecular OOF predictions,
full validity/ablation suite and all 14 report figures have been regenerated.
The old release has not been overwritten and remains an audit snapshot only.

1. Evidence-based exclusions, selected before viewing their effect on scores
   - 7 water labels: NIST exp_5 is a theoretical comparison, not experiment.
   - 330 curve labels: six esters have ambiguous isomer-trace correspondence.
     Their independent fixed-energy and published table peak labels remain.
   - 13 ozone labels: exp_2 is a renormalised version of the Newson experiment,
     not an independent experimental series. Retained: exp_1 Newson 10 points
     and exp_3 Siegel 9 points.
   Total: 350 curve observations quarantined, not erased from the audit trail.

2. Corrections to retained labels
   - All 15 Hudson Table 1 peak-height targets are unchanged and verified.
   - Table 1 supplies no peak energy: energy_eV is blank and its flag is false.
   - Uncertainty is 5% of the published height, using the source's stated accuracy.
   - Nine other ester curves remain as peak-anchored figure-trace extractions;
     they are not described as independent author-supplied numeric tables.
   - Water and ozone primary-region bounds are recalculated from retained data.
     Ozone peak boundary is 90.8947 eV; original CSV line 2866 at 100.499 eV is
     no longer a primary-region point. Water flags do not change.

3. Final dataset
   3,533 observations, 194 distinct identities.
   Curve: 3,316 observations / 74 identities.
   Fixed or sparse: 149 observations / 143 identities.
   Peak: 68 observations / 68 identities.
   Identity classes overlap; their counts must not be added to infer a total.
   Original outer folds are retained: all-row loads 722, 654, 667, 721, 769;
   curve identity counts 15, 14, 14, 15, 16.

4. Method policy
   The original 49 features, candidate models, seeds, nested selection and
   correction bounds remain. No optimisation was performed to recover an old
   favourable score. Sample weights were recalculated. All three heads were
   retrained, including fixed/peak heads whose inputs were affected by corrected
   identity-level BEB shape descriptors and missing peak-energy metadata.

5. Corrected results (molecule-macro primary-region NMAE)
   Main curve result: raw 15.27643228%, routed ML 14.16909467%.
   Absolute improvement 1.10733761 percentage points; relative 7.24866638%.
   Conditional molecule-bootstrap 95% CI: -0.61207425 to +2.69432228 pp.
   Positive differences in 18,062 of 20,000 resamples. The interval crosses zero:
   do not call this a statistically robust curve improvement or a probability
   that the model will improve a future molecule.

   Source-ingestion holdout: raw 15.37811171%, ML 17.38153612%,
   worsening by 2.00342442 pp. It covers 73 of 74 curve identities; 2-butanol is
   excluded because its singleton batch fails the unchanged minimum size rule.
   Only one of seven held-out batches improves. The seven batches are mutually
   exclusive ingestion groups, not seven fully independent experimental papers.

   Supplementary all-evidence result: raw 19.73753715%, ML 13.63989819%.
   Absolute improvement 6.09763896 pp; relative 30.89361614%.
   This combines curve, fixed/sparse and peak tasks and is not a 194-curve score.

6. Ablations
   Curve residual vs direct-log prediction: observed advantage 1.62335 pp,
   conditional paired 95% CI [0.56641, 2.87570] pp.
   Removing BEB-shape features: observed advantage 0.08619 pp,
   CI [-0.31116, 0.50259] pp; no clear advantage established.
   Pooled vs routed curve task: observed advantage 1.78585 pp,
   CI [0.46487, 3.25822] pp. This is a reported ablation; the headline model was
   not switched after observing it. It needs prospective independent evaluation.
   Pooled vs routed all evidence: advantage 1.09033 pp,
   CI [-0.13385, 2.62529] pp. These intervals are conditional on fixed OOF fits.

7. What changed in the report and reproducibility artifacts
   Every count, evidence-code table, source-batch size, fold table, fitted-model
   configuration, OOF point, metric, confidence interval, case ranking and figure
   must come from v2 outputs. The old 15.33% to 12.18% and 20.00% to 12.49%
   results are superseded and may appear only in explicitly marked audit history.
   Existing source and model limitations are not removed by this correction.
   Cluster bootstrap, repeated outer folds and a new independent dataset were
   not added; these must not be claimed as completed.

8. Reproduction and integrity
   Python 3.12.14; numerical package versions pinned in requirements.txt.
   The main run took 60.26 s; validity/ablation took 122.88 s on this workstation.
   The standard-library verifier checks the 3,883 = 3,533 + 350 row partition,
   the unchanged identity folds/retained targets, 15 corrected peak metadata rows,
   CSV-to-OOF positional alignment, and independently recomputed primary metrics.
   Final checksums describe exact deliverable bytes, not a proof of source truth.
   Raw QEC/Molpro reruns and full raw-PDF-to-CSV extraction are outside this
   numerical-release replay; the source ledger describes the remaining limits.
   A full separate-directory local rerun then reproduced all 81,259 OOF cells
   and 968 non-runtime JSON values (largest numeric cell difference 7.11e-15).
   This establishes local numerical replay, not independent external validation.
