INTERPRETATION AND LIMITS
The primary curve gain is modest and its conditional interval crosses zero.
The negative source-batch result does not support reliable new-source
generalisation. Current performance does not establish a universal improvement
over BEB. Source and chemical-family correlations, adaptive historical model
development, and incomplete raw-source reconstruction remain limitations.
The numerical-carrier audit checked all 125 adopted fixed-energy compilation
values against the actual experimental-mean column with zero discrepancies;
this is not an independent reconstruction of every original experiment.

Reproducibility, source-ledger, figure and checksum checks are reported separately.
Historical inputs, metrics and tags are preserved for audit, not reused as
current results.
