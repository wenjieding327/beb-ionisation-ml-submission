FINAL BOUNDED PRIMARY-SOURCE / NUMERICAL-CARRIER AUDIT
2026-09-06 — read-only audit of the retained 194-identity study, not an expansion of the dataset.

WHAT THIS AUDIT DOES AND DOES NOT CERTIFY

1. Bibliographic existence: an article/book/thesis and its identifier are real. This alone does NOT establish that it supports a particular claim or number.
2. Claim support: the inspected abstract/text/table/legend supports the statement attached to that citation, within its stated scope.
3. Numerical-carrier verification: the actual table/author database/figure-series from which the adopted number was recovered is identified and checked. This can be a later comparison paper or a standardized compilation.
4. Original-measurement reconstruction: each original experiment, calibration, digitization and uncertainty is independently reproduced. This has NOT been done for every row/source in the study. Do not claim that it has.

The revised paper should describe sources with these distinctions. A numerical value explicitly quoted in a later published comparison may be used with that comparison identified as the carrier and the earlier experiment identified as the original source. It must not be presented as an independent extraction from an unread original experimental paper.

A. BOSE STANDARDIZED FIXED-ENERGY EXPERIMENTAL LABELS — 125/125 CURRENT ROWS

Actual carrier: Arnab Bose, Polysaccharide and Heat-Transfer-Fluid Pyrolysis Mechanisms Developed Using Data, Theory, and Machine Learning, dissertation (2021), Appendix A, Table A.7, PDF pp.329–355 / printed pp.287–313.
Public original university repository:
https://repository.lib.ncsu.edu/bitstreams/e76df401-02d2-4db1-903b-dad1b9e89fe0/download
Associated article, not the thesis page locator: https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpca.0c06308

The heading and columns explicitly identify cross-section population means, interlab counts, and a 95% confidence-interval column. Section 2.3.1 (PDF pp.58–59 / printed pp.16–17) describes laboratory experimental values standardized to a common reference. Table A.2 (PDF pp.316–318 / printed pp.274–276) supplies dataset scaling factors. These are experimental-derived standardized means, NOT BEB/ML prediction columns.

The actual PDF mean column was replayed for 396 rows. The 125 retained training rows from ready100 (96), ALeCS approved manifests (23), and Zhong-Bose (6) were then checked by explicit source name/alias, molecular composition and table-record ID. All 125 experimental labels exactly equal the corresponding PDF Mean cross section values; zero value mismatches. None of these 125 mean entries has the original table's italic nonlinearity flag. This 125-row grouping does not claim to exhaust all other retained rows whose distant provenance includes Bose.

Machine-readable evidence: bose125_numeric_carrier_column_audit.json.
Method limitation: the existing PDF coordinate extractor was replayed; this is not an independently designed second digitization algorithm, nor a reconstruction of every source laboratory's raw data, standardization factor, mean or confidence interval.

Additional primary checks:
- Acetaldehyde: Table A.7 PDF p.337 / printed p.295, 8.3 A^2 and CI-column 3.6, N=4. Own-lab calibration is documented in Table B.2 PDF p.371 / printed p.329. All four original contributing measurements have NOT been independently reconstructed. Do not replace the four-source mean by one guessed original DOI.
- Acetic acid: Table A.7 PDF p.339 / printed p.297 explicitly says one source “our lab”, 10.3 A^2 and CI-column 1.6. The 70 eV measurement/calibration report is in Sections 2.2.1/B.3 and Table B.2. The original source here includes Bose's own measurement, not an absent earlier external paper. Raw chromatograms and uncertainty propagation were not independently recalculated.
- Propylcyanide/butyronitrile and isopropylcyanide: Table A.7 PDF p.347 / printed p.305, 16.3 and 15.3 A^2. The table prints “Zhu”, while the reference/scaling table resolves this to Zhou et al. 2019, https://doi.org/10.1080/00268976.2019.1583389. Original paper Table 2 and Figure 3 distinguish the two structures and measured curves. Bose applies factor 1.40 to the 70 eV dataset; the adopted values remain Bose-standardized, not this project's freshly digitized original ordinates.

Required wording boundary: “source-harmonized 70–75 eV experimental population mean”, not “an experimental average of measured values at exactly 70 and 75 eV for each molecule”. The model's BEB endpoint-mean approximation is a separate modelling choice.

REMAINING LIMITATIONS / NON-BLOCKING SOURCE STATUS

- NIST series provenance has been restored to the actual experimental series used, but not every older original experimental paper has been independently read in full. Carbonyl sulfide includes a private-communication source carried by NIST; there is no original public paper to pretend to have verified for that series.
- The 125-row Bose table transfer is verified; source-specific means/scaling/CI calculations are adopted from Bose, not independently reconstructed. The multi-laboratory source trail for every mean is not complete at the raw-data level.
- Later theoretical comparisons sometimes carry the only accessible numerical experimental maxima/curves. References to original experiments establish provenance, not a false claim that every primary full text or every ordinate was independently re-extracted.
- Nine retained ester curves are graph-marker extractions anchored to Table 1, not independent author-supplied numerical experimental tables. The six ambiguous ester curves and the identified NIST theory/renormalized duplicates remain excluded by the earlier 350-row source audit.
- No outcome-based exclusions or baseline changes are authorized by this note. Source/data semantics, not better ML scores, motivated the listed corrections.
